Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 9/24/25

Last updated: November 7, 2026 (contact details added, extension name matched to the Chrome Web Store, and the description of what is saved corrected)

We respect your privacy. The Visual Timer by Lesson Shuffle Chrome Extension is designed to function entirely on your local device.

Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information, browsing history, or usage data. The extension does not use analytics, trackers, or third-party integrations.

How the Extension Works

Timer settings (presets, theme and colors, end sound, volume, the time-is-up message, the flash option, and which edge the Timer tab sits on) are saved in Chrome’s extension storage. If you use Chrome Sync, Chrome may copy these settings to your Google account.

The last time you used, the position of the timer and its tab, and any sounds you add yourself are saved in Chrome’s extension storage on your computer only.

Visual Timer makes no network requests, and no information is sent to external servers.

Visual Timer has no online features.

Third-Party Sharing

We do not share, sell, or disclose data to any third parties because no user data is collected.

Children’s Privacy

The extension is safe for use in educational settings, including with children under 13, because it does not collect or transmit any data.
