WebInk Legal

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 10/08/2025

Last updated: November 7, 2026 (contact details added, and the description of what is saved corrected)

We respect your privacy. The WebInk by Lesson Shuffle Chrome Extension is designed to function entirely on your local device.

Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information, browsing history, or usage data. The extension does not use analytics, trackers, or third-party integrations.

How the Extension Works

Your drawings, highlights and erasing exist only on the page you are viewing, in the browser’s memory, and are lost when you reload or close the page. Toolbar preferences (colors, tool sizes, layout and toolbar position) are saved in Chrome’s extension storage. If you use Chrome Sync, Chrome may copy the color and size preferences to your Google account.

Screenshots you create are saved to your device as downloaded image files.

WebInk makes no network requests, and no information is sent to external servers.

WebInk has no online features.

Third-Party Sharing

We do not share, sell, or disclose data to any third parties because no user data is collected.

Children’s Privacy

The extension is safe for use in educational settings, including with children under 13, because it does not collect or transmit any data.
