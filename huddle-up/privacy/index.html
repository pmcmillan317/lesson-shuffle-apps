Huddle Up Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 25, 2026

Huddle Up by Lesson Shuffle is a classroom review game that runs in a web browser at apps.lessonshuffle.com. It is designed so that no information about students is collected or kept. This policy explains exactly what happens to the information that passes through the game.

Who uses it

A teacher opens a game room on the teacher page (/huddle-up/host/). Students join the room on their own devices on the join page (/join/) by typing a room code and a nickname. Neither teachers nor students create an account or sign in.

What students enter

  • A room code, shown on the teacher's screen.
  • A nickname. Students are asked to use a nickname or first name only. The teacher can choose to give every student a random name instead, or replace any name with a random one.
  • Their answers (which answer button they tap) during the game.
  • Terms they suggest, only if the teacher turns on “Students write the set.”

We do not ask for, and the game has no place to enter, a full name, email address, student ID, age, school or password.

Where that information goes

  • The game runs in the teacher's browser tab. Nicknames, teams, scores and suggested terms are held there while the game is open. If the teacher refreshes the page, the browser's session storage lets the game pick up where it left off. That copy stays in that browser tab and is deleted when the teacher closes the room or closes the tab.
  • Students' devices and the teacher's tab talk through a small relay service run by Lesson Shuffle at rooms.lessonshuffle.com, on Cloudflare. The relay passes each message along as it happens and does not save it. It does not write nicknames, answers, scores or suggested terms to any database or file, and it does not log the content of messages.
  • To route messages, the relay keeps in memory a random code for each connected device. These codes are made up by the browser, are not linked to any person, and disappear when the device disconnects.
  • The only thing the relay stores is the room's random teacher key and the time the room was opened, so that only the teacher's screen can run the room. Both are deleted when the teacher closes the room, 15 minutes after everyone has left, or 4 hours after the room opened, whichever comes first.
  • A student's device keeps its own random device code and nickname in that browser tab's session storage, so a refresh rejoins the same team. It is deleted when the tab is closed or the game ends.

Term lists

Term lists are entered by the teacher. A teacher who presses “Save this list in this browser” saves the list in their own browser's local storage on their own computer. It is never sent to Lesson Shuffle. The terms in the current game are sent to students' devices through the relay so they can play, and are not saved by the relay.

Page counts

The join page that students use has no analytics and loads nothing from other sites. The teacher pages and help pages use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts page views without cookies, and send anonymous click counts (a label and the page address, nothing about the visitor) to lessonshuffle.com, like the rest of this site.

Cookies, ads and selling

Huddle Up sets no cookies and shows no ads. We do not sell, rent or share any information, and there is no student information for us to sell or share.

Service providers

The website and the relay are hosted by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare handles network traffic, including IP addresses, in order to deliver pages and messages and to protect against attacks. Lesson Shuffle does not receive or keep IP addresses from the relay.

Children and schools

Huddle Up is made for classrooms, including students under 13. Because it collects no personal information from students, it does not create student records. Schools and districts decide for themselves which tools they use. We are glad to answer questions from a district's data privacy officer, including for New York Education Law 2-d review.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
