Huddle Up

A team review game where nobody can win alone. Paste your terms, pick a way to play, and open a room.

1 Your terms

One pair on each line. Put a tab, a comma, a colon or a dash between the term and its match. A list copied from a spreadsheet, or exported from most flashcard tools, works as it is.

    Add a picture to any match. Students see it with the meaning, so it helps with science diagrams, maps, art and early readers. Pictures are shrunk and kept in this browser, and only go to students' screens during the game.
